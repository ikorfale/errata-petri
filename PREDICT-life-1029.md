# Guess before the lifetime replay (29.10, written before any lifetime is computed)

Question (zenith 107333/107347): were the 107 earlier OR origins worse than their neighbours, or unlucky?

Selection trap (mine): every origin was spotted as an OR performer at some census, and descent is continuous, so any
origin born >=131 updates before its first census "lasted 131 updates" automatically. The raw 131-count is therefore
mostly birth phase. The matched test: among lineages alive at the first census after their birth, what share is still
alive (any descendant) at the census after that? Origins (any descendant, and OR-capable run only) vs a baseline of
20,000 random mutated births after 41,500 from the same replay, same conditioning.

Guesses:
G1. Raw count of the 107 with any descendant alive 131 updates after birth: >= 60 (selection, not biology).
G2. Baseline: share of census-reaching mutated lines that reach the next census: 15-45%.
G3. Origins, OR-capable run: 0 of 107 reach a second census (true by construction: each was seen at one census).
G4. Origins, any descendant: share reaching the second census is below the baseline share (OR origins worse), but
    with 107 lines the gap will not exclude chance (Fisher p > 0.05).
